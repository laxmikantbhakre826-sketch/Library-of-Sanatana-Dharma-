# Introduction to Sanatana Dharma

Sanatana Dharma means Eternal Duty. It is not just a religion, it is a way of life.

## What is Dharma?
Dharma means doing right thing at right time - for yourself, family and society.

## 4 Main Pillars
1. Satya - Truth
2. Ahimsa - Non-violence
3. Dharma - Duty
4. Seva - Service

## Main Books in this Library
- Ramayana - Story of Rama
- Mahabharat - Story of Pandavas
- Bhagavad Gita - Krishna's Teaching
- Vedas - Knowledge
- Puranas - Stories of Gods
- Upanishads - Philosophy

Start your journey with Ramayana or Bhagavad Gita.
